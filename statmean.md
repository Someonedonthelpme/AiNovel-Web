Strength (STR): Measures physical power and athletic ability.
Dexterity (DEX): Increases accuracy (HIT), stabilizes physical weapon damage, and reduces magic casting time.
Constitution (CON): maximum Hit Points (HP) and your ability to resist poisons, disease, or maintain focus on spells.
Agility (AGI): Increases attack speed (ASPD) and improves your flee rate (evasion).
Vitality (VIT): Increases maximum HP, accelerates HP recovery speed, and raises physical defense (DEF). It also provides resistance to negative statuses like Stun.						
Intelligence (INT): Measures reasoning, memory, and book learning.
Wisdom (WIS): Measures perception, insight, and mental awareness.
Charisma (CHA): Measures confidence, poise, and social influence.
Luck (LUK): Increases critical hit chance (CRIT), influent dice roll, more/ great loot/ activite.
